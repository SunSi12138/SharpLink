## Claim and cause selection

A public application-owned TimeProvider may throw during disconnect cleanup. The existing queued ClientConnection.Fail/FailAll is not a synchronous barrier before the send pump's real pending-table observer. Clock failure must not leave pending slots/operations, later physical frames, either queue, flush markers or capacity waiters behind. The authoritative transport exception remains the operation/flush terminal cause when deadline expiry cannot be sampled. A successfully sampled expired deadline still wins. Clock/observer cleanup failures must surface from joined cleanup, not be silently discarded or substituted for a known transport cause. No elapsed duration is invented for a failed admission-outcome timestamp.
